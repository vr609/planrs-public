🔍 WHAT IS A PIXEL?
A pixel is the tiniest coloured square on any screen.

Millions of them sit side-by-side to make every photo, video, and emoji you see!

🦒 Just like a giraffe's spots are made of patches of colour — a screen is made of millions of pixel patches!
