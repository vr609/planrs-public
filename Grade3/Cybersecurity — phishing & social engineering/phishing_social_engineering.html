🏷️ Warning Labels

Sneaky zero trick

Scammers replace letters with numbers: "g00gle" uses zeros instead of 'o'!

🆘

False urgency

Real services don't threaten to delete your account in 30 minutes to force you to act.

🎀

Fake prizes

Winning something you never entered is a classic bait to steal your details.

🤫

Secrecy requests

Asking you to keep a secret or act alone stops you getting help from a trusted adult.
