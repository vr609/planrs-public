Series Circuit — LEDs in a Chain

1 Build the Series Circuit

Drag each component from the bank below into the correct slot in the circuit. In a series circuit, all components sit in ONE single path — current flows through them one after another! 🔗

2 Find the Broken LED

In a series circuit, if ONE LED breaks, ALL go dark. Click the faulty (dim) LED to fix the chain! Each round a random LED goes bad. Can you score 5 out of 5? 💡

3 Sort the Circuit Steps
